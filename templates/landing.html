QC / THE STAMP DECIDES

Three grades,
three fates.

A
100%

Back on the shelf.

Sealed, tagged, spotless. It's restocked the same day and the customer gets every rupee back.

B
70%

Off to the bench.

Scuffed or missing a tag. A technician fixes it, QC checks it again, and 70% goes back once it passes.

C
0%

End of the line.

Beyond repair, so it's scrapped. If it arrived damaged or was the wrong item, that's on us: full refund anyway.
